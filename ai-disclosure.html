2. The Provider Chain

  • Your device prepares the payload. Text recognition (OCR) and the first pass of date detection happen locally, with no network connection.
  • Our proxy, operated by us and hosted on Cloudflare, receives the payload over HTTPS. It is authenticated and accepts requests only from the App. It does not retain request or response bodies.
  • Google runs the Gemini model that produces the output, under its API terms and Gemini data-use policies. We use the Gemini API rather than a consumer surface; this is the path on which Google's documented terms state that submitted content is not used to train its models.

We do not control these third parties and do not assume their obligations. A change in their terms or behaviour may affect what happens to content you submit. We may change AI providers at any time, on notice through an update to this Disclosure.

3. The Five AI Features

3.1 AutoName — suggests a document title

Transmits: one page image from the document, downscaled before sending, together with the recognised text of the document.
Runs: automatically after each scan finishes processing, and whenever you re-run it manually.
Limits: free tier has a weekly allowance (extendable by watching a rewarded ad); Pro is unlimited.

3.2 AutoSort — suggests a category folder

Transmits: nothing additional — AutoSort is part of the same single request as AutoName.
Runs: alongside AutoName. The suggested category is applied automatically for Pro subscribers.

3.3 Recall — ask questions about your documents

Transmits: your question, plus the title and an excerpt of the recognised text from up to five documents the App judges most relevant to it, plus recent messages from the current conversation so the model can follow context. If you dictate your question, see Section 6 — the audio may be processed by Apple.
Runs: when you send a message.
Limits: free tier has a weekly allowance (extendable by watching a rewarded ad); Pro is unlimited.

3.4 Key dates — finds due dates, expiry and renewal dates

Transmits: the recognised text of the document, plus today's date. This is the largest text payload of any feature — it needs to see much more of the document than naming does, because deadlines hide deep in the page.
Runs: automatically, in the background. The App first tries to find dates entirely on your device; only when that finds nothing does it send the text for AI extraction. On the free tier this applies to your two oldest documents; for Pro subscribers it applies to all documents.
Limits: deliberately does not consume your AutoName or Recall allowance.

3.5 Recall suggestions — proposes questions you might ask

Transmits: the title, category, and a short text excerpt from each of your three most recent documents.
Runs: automatically, once your library reaches three documents, and is refreshed periodically after that.
Limits: no allowance is consumed and there is no separate control for this feature.

4. Which Features Run Without Asking You

This is important enough to state on its own. AutoName, AutoSort, key-date extraction, and Recall suggestions all run automatically — after a scan, or in the background as your library grows. They do not prompt you each time. Only Recall itself is triggered by an explicit action every time.

There is no in-app switch to disable AI processing. NativeScan is designed and sold as an AI-assisted document scanner; the AI is not an add-on. Your acceptance of this Disclosure during onboarding is your consent to all of the processing described in Section 3. If you do not want a particular document's contents transmitted for AI processing, do not scan that document into NativeScan.

5. What is Not Transmitted

  • Documents in your library are only transmitted when one of the features in Section 3 runs. Content is not continuously uploaded, and nothing is uploaded merely because it is stored in the App.
  • Your real name, email address, Apple ID, or location is never attached to an AI request by us.
  • The anonymous analytics identifier described in the Privacy Policy is not sent with AI requests. AI requests cannot be linked back to an install or to each other.
  • Biometric data (Face ID / Touch ID / Optic ID) is handled entirely by iOS and never leaves your device.
  • Neither we nor our proxy retain your documents, images, recognised text, questions, or the AI's answers after a request completes.

6. Voice Input

If you dictate a Recall question instead of typing it, the App uses Apple's speech-recognition framework. Depending on your device, language, and iOS settings, Apple may process that audio on Apple's servers rather than on your device. That processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours. We never receive the audio — only the resulting text, which is then transmitted as part of your Recall question. To keep audio entirely off the network, type your questions instead.

A date the App shows you may be misread, misinterpreted, or attached to the wrong obligation — and a real deadline in your document may be missed entirely.
